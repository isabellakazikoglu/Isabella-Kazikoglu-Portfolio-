Financial Statement Analysis

Kimberly-Clark Financial Statement Analysis

An Excel-based analysis of Kimberly-Clark Corporation’s consolidated financial statements using financial information obtained from filings available through the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

View Excel Workbook View Project Report

Project Overview

For this project, I collected financial statement information from Kimberly-Clark Corporation’s SEC filings and organized the data in Microsoft Excel. I then used the financial information to conduct ratio analysis, vertical analysis, and horizontal analysis.

I also reviewed selected financial statement disclosures and explained how the company accounts for revenue recognition, advertising expenses, research and development costs, cash equivalents, and leases.

Project Objective

The objective of this project was to apply financial accounting concepts to the published financial statements of a public company. I used the analysis to evaluate changes in profitability, debt levels, cash flow, and financial statement activity across multiple reporting periods.

Data Source

The financial information used in this project was collected from Kimberly-Clark Corporation filings available through the SEC’s EDGAR database. I transferred the relevant income statement, balance sheet, cash flow statement, and stockholders’ equity information into Excel for analysis.

Tools and Skills Used

Microsoft Excel SEC EDGAR Research Financial Statement Analysis Ratio Analysis Horizontal Analysis Vertical Analysis Accounting Disclosures Financial Interpretation

Analysis Performed

Ratio Analysis

I calculated and compared financial ratios for 2023 and 2024, including profit margin, debt-to-assets, and free cash flow.

Vertical Analysis

I analyzed the 2024 balance sheet by presenting individual account balances as percentages of total assets.

Horizontal Analysis

I compared income statement activity across 2022, 2023, and 2024 to identify changes in financial performance over time.

Financial Statement Notes

I reviewed selected accounting disclosures and translated the company’s accounting policies into straightforward explanations.

Key Findings

Profitability Improved

Kimberly-Clark’s profit margin increased from approximately 9% in 2023 to approximately 14% in 2024. The increase was supported by a substantial rise in net income.

Debt-to-Assets Remained Stable

The company’s debt-to-assets ratio remained approximately 94% in both 2023 and 2024, indicating that the proportion of assets financed through liabilities did not change significantly.

Free Cash Flow Declined

Calculated free cash flow declined from approximately $1.188 billion in 2023 to approximately $885 million in 2024.

Financial Statement Notes Reviewed

  • Revenue recognition and the timing of recording product sales
  • Advertising expenses and when campaign costs are recognized
  • Research and development costs expensed as incurred
  • The classification of short-term investments as cash equivalents
  • Recognition of lease assets and lease liabilities

What I Learned

This project strengthened my ability to locate financial information in SEC filings, organize large amounts of accounting data, perform financial analysis in Excel, and explain what financial results mean in clear language.

It also gave me experience connecting financial statement numbers with the accounting policies and disclosures that support them.
